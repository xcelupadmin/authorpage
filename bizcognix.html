BizCogniX

See where AI pays off — a curated library of AI case studies from leading companies across industries, processes, and economies.

The Idea

Where AI is already proving its value

BizCogniX shows business leaders where AI is creating measurable value — and how leading companies made it happen. It is a curated library of case studies drawn from companies across industries, business processes, and the world's major economies.

Each case is compiled from public sources and links back to them.

For Business Leaders

It is built for CXOs and business leaders who need to decide where AI belongs in their organization. With BizCogniX you can:

Benchmark your peers

See what comparable companies in your industry have deployed.

Spot where AI delivers ROI

Compare reported results across processes and markets.

Prioritize investments

Focus on the processes where AI is proving its value.

Learn from proven implementations

Understand the approach, timeline, and technology behind each result.

Inside Each Case

Every case is held to the same outline, so one implementation can be read beside another. It covers what was implemented, the timeline, the investment where disclosed, the technologies used, and the measured results, with links to the original sources.

Figures are stated only as the cited sources report them. Where an investment or a result is not disclosed, the case says so. The library is updated regularly as new cases are published.

Across Industries and Processes

Cases are organized by industry group and by the business process they primarily address — so a leader can start from a market they know, or from a function they need to improve.

Industry groups

Brewing & Beverages · Food & Consumer Goods · Logistics & 3PL · Wholesale & Distribution · Retail · Technology · Manufacturing & Automotive · Pharmaceuticals

Business processes

Demand Planning & Forecasting · Supply & Production Planning · Procurement & Sourcing · Manufacturing & Quality · Maintenance & Asset Management · Inventory & Warehousing · Logistics & Transportation · Customer & Order Management · Sustainability & Energy · Finance & Freight Payment

Economies

Cases are drawn from companies operating in the world's major economies, including work that spans more than one country.
