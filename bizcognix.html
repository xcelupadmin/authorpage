The Library

BizCogniX

See where AI pays off. Proven AI stories from leading companies around the world, gathered in one place, so you can learn from what is already working instead of starting from scratch.

The Idea

Proof, not promises

Every week, companies somewhere in the world put AI to work and report real results. Those stories are scattered across news releases, earnings calls, and industry reports. BizCogniX brings them together in one place, so leaders can see what is working, in their own industry and beyond, without hunting for it.

Each story links back to its original public source.

For Business Leaders

It is built for CXOs and business leaders deciding where AI belongs in their organization. With BizCogniX you can:

See what your peers are doing

Find what comparable companies in your industry have already put into practice.

Spot where AI is paying off

See which processes and markets are reporting real results.

Focus your next move

Put your attention where AI is already proving itself.

Learn from proven stories

Understand what was done, the technology behind it, and the results reported.

Inside Each Story

Each story follows the same simple outline, so one can be read beside another: what the company did, the technology behind it, and the results it reported, with links to the original sources.

Results are stated only as the sources report them. New stories are added as they are published, so the library keeps pace with where AI is delivering.

Across Industries and Processes

Stories are organized by industry group and by the business process they primarily address — so a leader can start from a market they know, or from a function they need to improve.
