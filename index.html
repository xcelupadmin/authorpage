The Book

Some symbols survive for centuries.
But do we still know how to see them?

Close Your Eyes to See is a reflective journey into India's ancient temples, their repeating forms, and the questions they continue to awaken — a book about memory, meaning, and how civilizations preserve knowledge across fragile generations.

Intercognix

The unknown between the known

Beyond the book, Jay Gontu coined the term Intercognix and developed it into a practice for finding discoveries hidden in the gaps between fields that have never spoken to each other. Each candidate pairs two independently known ideas, names the principle that bridges them, and states the test that could prove it wrong.

Bizcognix

Stay ahead of what moves your industry

Bizcognix turns the daily flow of business updates from around the world into clear intelligence: proven AI stories, emerging trends, and what leading companies are doing across industries and processes. The Author

Writing between two worlds of knowledge

Jay Gontu is an author and technologist based in Chicago, drawn to a single question: how do civilizations remember? His work connects the systems that store knowledge today with the stone, symbol, and ritual that carried it for millennia — and, through Intercognix, looks for what lies between the things we already know.
